Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about billing, data accuracy guarantees, and onboarding.

How does the 7-day free trial and refund guarantee work?
You get full, immediate access to test the platform for 7 days. If LeadintelAI doesn't directly accelerate your outbound deal pipeline, simply cancel before the trial concludes and you will not be charged a single rupee. Every paid tier also includes our 98% data delivery guarantee with automatic instant credit refunds on any invalid records.
How does Meta's conversation pricing apply to the WhatsApp Suite?
Our subscription fees cover the LeadintelAI autonomous software engine, Maya AI bot orchestration, multi-agent chat, and Meta Cloud API integration. Meta charges a standard per-conversation rate (typically ₹0.35–₹0.80 per 24-hour service or marketing window in India) which is billed directly through your Meta Business Manager payment method without any markup from our side.
Can I switch between individual modular tools and the All-in-One Engine?
Yes. You can start with a single tool (e.g. DataCloud for verified contacts) and seamlessly upgrade to the All-in-One Revenue Engine as your SDR and sales floor expands. Any unused credits or subscription days from your modular plan are automatically credited pro-rata toward your new bundle.
How does MailEngine protect my domain reputation and prevent spam folders?
MailEngine utilizes our automated peer-to-peer inbox warm-up network combined with multi-domain rotation. Rather than sending 5,000 emails from your primary corporate domain, MailEngine safely distributes volume across isolated secondary inboxes (with proper SPF, DKIM, and DMARC alignment), ensuring your deliverability stays consistently above 95%.
How fresh is the B2B contact data and what compliance standards apply?
Our contact intelligence is verified dynamically across public registries, MCA director databases, and live SMTP/telco handshake validation. Data older than 30 days is automatically requalified before being served to you. All operations are strictly aligned with India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 and GDPR enterprise standards.
Do you provide GST tax invoices and custom enterprise MSAs?
Yes. Input your company GSTIN during checkout to receive automated compliant 18% GST tax invoices for full input tax credit (ITC). For mid-market and enterprise teams requiring custom procurement Master Service Agreements (MSAs), custom DPAs, or purchase order (PO) billing, our enterprise sales team can accommodate your legal workflow.
