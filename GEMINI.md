## Non-Negotiable Visual Standards
- **Zero Neon**: No `#4ade80`, `#22c55e`, or glowing indicator lights. Replace with solid Deep Forest Green (`#065f46`, border `#047857`, text `#34d399` on dark canvas / `#ffffff` on fill, tint `rgba(6, 95, 70, 0.16)`).
- **Zero Pastel / Cyan / Yellow**: Replace light blue (`#93c5fd`), cyan (`#38bdf8`), light amber/yellow (`#fbbf24`, `#f59e0b`), and pastel red (`#f87171`) with Deep Navy (`#1e3a8a`), Deep Slate (`#0f172a`), Rich Saffron (`#ea580c`, `#c2410c`, text `#fb923c` on MailEngine), and Deep Crimson (`#991b1b`).
- **Zero AI Gimmicks**: No purple gradients (`#7c3aed`, `#6366f1`), no gradient text spans, no excessive `backdrop-filter: blur`, no glowing box shadows. Use solid dark surfaces (`#0a0d14`, `#0f1420`) and crisp 1px borders.
- **Zero Emojis & Tinted Boxes**: Strip interface emojis from headings and buttons. Icons must sit bare on transparent backgrounds without tinted backdrop squares.
- **Zero Bouncy Hover**: No `scale(1.05)` or bouncy spring transitions. Use clean 150ms opacity/border transitions.
- **Preserve Structure**: Never touch backend APIs, routing, DOM hierarchy, layout spacing, or marketing copy.
