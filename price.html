Frequently Asked Questions

How fresh is the B2B data?
Our data is continuously verified and updated in real-time. We parse professional networks and validate email and phone endpoints to ensure a bounce rate consistently below 5%.
Is the WhatsApp API reliable and official?
Yes, our infrastructure integrates strictly with the Meta Official WhatsApp Business API. Your number is protected from algorithmic bans, ensuring safe, large-scale broadcast messaging.
Can I map my own domains for Mass Mailing?
Absolutely. Our platform supports robust custom domain setups. By fully authenticating your domains (DKIM, SPF, DMARC), you establish solid sender reputation and maximize inbox deliverability.
Are there setup fees or hidden costs?
No hidden setup fees. Our pricing plans include full access as listed. Bear in mind that Meta's standard conversation charges apply directly on top of your WhatsApp API subscription tier based on your outgoing volume.
