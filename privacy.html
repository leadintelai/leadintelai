Privacy Policy & Data Governance

Effective Date: April 1, 2026 • Jurisdiction: Republic of India • Regulatory Standard: DPDP Act 2023 & IT Act 2000

LeadintelAI Technologies Private Limited ("LeadintelAI", "we", "us", or "our") operates the LeadintelAI intelligence platform, DataCloud directory, AI CRM, WhatsApp Suite, and MailEngine. We believe that scalable B2B revenue execution must be grounded in radical transparency, uncompromising technical security, and strict statutory compliance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act 2023).

Section 01

Scope & Role Definitions

This Privacy Policy governs the processing of information accessed, collected, or processed through LeadintelAI web applications, APIs, Chrome extensions, and outreach infrastructure.

Under the DPDP Act 2023:

  • LeadintelAI as Data Fiduciary: We act as a Data Fiduciary regarding user account credentials, operator authentication logs, customer billing metadata, and proprietary usage telemetry.
  • LeadintelAI as Data Processor: When you import your own prospect lists or run WhatsApp Suite and MailEngine cadences to external decision-makers, you act as the primary Data Fiduciary and LeadintelAI processes data strictly on your operational instruction.
  • B2B Contact Telemetry: All professional intelligence presented within DataCloud pertains exclusively to individuals in their official corporate capacity (Directors, Founders, CXOs, and business department heads) and is compiled solely for legitimate business-to-business engagement.
Section 02

Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum necessary information required to deliver enterprise sales execution capabilities:

  • Customer Account Data: Name, work email address, phone number, company name, corporate billing address, and GSTIN for tax invoice generation.
  • Public Professional B2B Records: Publicly registered Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) corporate filings, professional social network profiles (LinkedIn), company press releases, and corporate WHOIS records.
  • Telemetry & Usage Logs: IP address, browser user-agent, session authentication timestamps, API request volumes, and feature interaction metrics.
  • Outreach Performance Metrics: Aggregate email delivery rates, bounce signals, WhatsApp message status receipts (Sent, Delivered, Read), and campaign response velocity.
Strict Exclusion of Sensitive Personal Data LeadintelAI never collects, processes, or indexes sensitive personal data such as Aadhaar numbers, PAN cards, biometric data, personal banking credentials, passwords, religious affiliations, political opinions, or medical health records.
Section 03

DPDP Act 2023 Compliance & Legal Grounding

LeadintelAI's data sourcing, indexing, and enrichment practices adhere strictly to the principles codified in the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023:

  • Legitimate Certain Uses (Section 7): B2B corporate contact information is processed for specified, lawful business communications where individuals have made their professional credentials publicly accessible for commercial discourse.
  • Purpose Limitation: B2B data accessed through DataCloud may only be utilized for lawful commercial outreach, partnership exploration, or recruitment purposes. Unlawful harassment, phishing, or mass consumer spam is strictly prohibited.
  • Data Accuracy & Hygiene: Section 8 mandates that personal data processed is accurate and complete. LeadintelAI verifies contacts in real time and automatically deletes invalid or defunct records upon receipt of bounce signals.
Section 04

Telecom Line Status & SMTP Verification Standards

To maintain a sub-0.6% bounce rate and eliminate non-functional numbers, LeadintelAI conducts non-intrusive technical status pings:

  • Live Telecom Line Ping: Signals are sent to verify whether a corporate SIM card is actively provisioned with Indian telecom carriers. This check never intercepts telephone calls, accesses SMS messages, or captures private device data.
  • Triple SMTP Handshake: We ping recipient mail transfer agents (MX servers) to verify mailbox existence and detect catch-all configurations without delivering intrusive marketing messages during verification.
Section 05

Zero AI Model Training Guarantee

We recognize that your sales pipeline telemetry, deal stage notes, customized WhatsApp sales scripts, and closed revenue figures represent your proprietary enterprise intellectual property.

No Model Training on Customer CRM Data LeadintelAI guarantees that your private customer communications, internal deal commentary, custom prompt templates, and CRM data are strictly isolated. We do not use your private tenant data to train public foundation models or cross-customer algorithms.
Section 06

Tenant Isolation, Encryption & Security Architecture

LeadintelAI applies defense-in-depth infrastructure controls designed to satisfy SOC 2 and ISO/IEC 27001 parameters:

  • Data Sovereignty: All customer operational databases, activity records, and backups are hosted in secure AWS data centers situated in Mumbai, India (ap-south-1).
  • Cryptographic Standards: 256-bit AES encryption at rest across all persistent storage volumes; TLS 1.3 encryption for all data in transit across public networks.
  • Tenant-Level Isolation: Database access controls prevent any cross-tenant data contamination. Each customer organization accesses only its provisioned schema partitions.
  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Granular permissions permit administrative control over rep seats, export capabilities, and visibility scopes.
Section 07

User Rights, Opt-Out & 72-Hour Erasure

Every individual retains full autonomy over their professional directory listing under Section 12 of the DPDP Act 2023. We maintain an automated, frictionless opt-out engine:

  • Right of Access & Summary: Any professional may request a complete summary of data points associated with their name or corporate email within our directory.
  • Right to Correction & Erasure: Upon receipt of an opt-out request through our portal or via email to privacy@leadintelai.com, your professional record is permanently quarantined and expunged across all active query indexes within 72 hours.
  • Suppression Blacklist: To prevent accidental re-indexing from public registries, deleted identifiers are added to a non-queryable cryptographic suppression hash.
Section 08

Authorized Sub-Processors & Partners

We engage tier-1 infrastructure providers bound by rigorous Data Processing Agreements (DPAs):

  • Amazon Web Services (AWS Mumbai): Primary cloud compute, container orchestration, and encrypted database storage.
  • Meta Platforms Inc. (Meta Cloud API): Official enterprise API transmission for approved WhatsApp business messaging.
  • Razorpay & Stripe: PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant payment processing. We never store credit/debit card numbers on our infrastructure.
