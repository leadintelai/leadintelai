About Leadintel AI

Leadintel AI is an advanced B2B sales intelligence and communication platform designed to help businesses find prospects, engage decision-makers, and convert leads into customers — all from a single platform.

We provide high-quality B2B decision-maker data, including CXOs, CTOs, Marketing Heads, Sales Leaders, and other key professionals. Our platform helps companies discover the right contacts and build targeted outreach strategies.

In addition to lead data, Leadintel AI offers powerful outreach tools such as WhatsApp Business API and Mass Email Marketing, enabling businesses to directly connect with prospects and run large-scale campaigns.

By combining verified B2B data, WhatsApp automation, and email marketing tools, Leadintel AI enables companies to generate leads, nurture relationships, and drive sales efficiently.

This integrated approach makes Leadintel AI a complete B2B sales platform, helping businesses manage their prospecting, communication, and conversion workflows in one place.
