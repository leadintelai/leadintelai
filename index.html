FAQ

Questions we get all the time.

Is there a free trial? Do I need a credit card?

Yes — all plans include a 7-day free trial. No credit card is required to start. You only enter payment details when you decide to upgrade.

Can I use my existing WhatsApp Business number?

Yes. LeadintelAI connects to your existing WhatsApp Business number via the official WhatsApp Business API. Your number, your brand — just with automation, templates, and CRM sync added on top.

How is LeadintelAI different from a regular CRM like Selesforce or Hubspott?

LeadintelAI is purpose-built for Indian B2B sales teams. It combines a B2B lead database (DataCloud), native WhatsApp outreach, email sequences, and an AI CRM — all in one platform. Selesforce charges 3–5x more and requires separate tools for data, WhatsApp, and email.

Where does the lead data come from? Is it DPDP compliant?

Our DataCloud sources data from publicly available directories including IndiaMART, Justdial, LinkedIn, and company websites. All data is verified and refreshed regularly. LeadintelAI is fully compliant with India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time with no penalty. Annual plan holders retain access until the end of their billing period. You can export all your data before cancelling.

Does LeadintelAI work for small teams or solo founders?

Absolutely. The Starter plan is designed for solo founders and small teams (1 user). Many of our customers started as solo founders and expanded to team plans as they grew.
